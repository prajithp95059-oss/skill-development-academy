Formats & duration

Full-time, part-time, or self-paced — same curriculum, different pace

Every track can be studied in whichever format fits your schedule. Switching formats mid-cohort is a conversation with your advisor, not a re-enrollment.

Fastest route

Full-Time

30–35 hrs / week · Mon–Fri, daytime cohort

Live sessions every weekday, daily standups with your cohort, and same-day mentor office hours. Built for learners who can treat this like a job for the duration of the track.

Typical duration8–16 weeks
Mentor check-ins3× weekly
Capstone supportDedicated sprint week
Alongside your job

Part-Time

10–14 hrs / week · Evenings & weekends

The same modules, spread out so you can keep working or studying elsewhere. Live sessions are recorded, and mentor reviews happen on a rolling weekly cycle.

Typical duration16–28 weeks
Mentor check-ins1× weekly
Capstone supportSpread over 3 weeks
Your own timeline

Self-Paced

Flexible · No fixed weekly minimum

All lessons and briefs unlock upfront. You submit work whenever it's ready and book mentor review slots as you go — best for learners with unpredictable schedules.

After the capstone

A real internship, not a certificate of completion

Top-performing graduates from eligible tracks move straight into a paid or stipend-based internship with one of Stackly's partner companies — working on actual tickets, with an actual manager.

01

Capstone review & eligibility

Once your capstone is mentor-approved, it's scored against the internship rubric — no separate application needed for eligible tracks.

02

Partner matching

Our placements team matches you to 2–3 partner companies based on your track, capstone focus, and location preference.

03

Panel interview

A short technical + culture-fit conversation with the partner team — Stackly preps you with a mock round beforehand.

04

6–8 week internship

You work inside the partner's team on real deliverables, with a Stackly mentor still checking in weekly as a safety net.

05

Conversion or reference

Strong performers regularly receive a full-time offer; everyone leaves with a manager reference and a verified work sample.
